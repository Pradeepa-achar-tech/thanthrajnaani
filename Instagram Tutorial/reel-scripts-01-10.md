# LocalInsta Reels Series — "Build Instagram, FREE, No Card" 🎬

**10 × 30-second Instagram Reels scripts**, built from the actual LocalInsta course
(Flutter + Supabase). Target audience: Karnataka college students (BCA/BE/MCA, coastal
Karnataka flavour — Kundapura/Udupi/Mangalore energy). Tone: fast, funny, proud-local,
zero jargon-dump — every technical fact is real (pulled straight from the course), just
said the way you'd explain it to a junior at a canteen table, not a classroom.

This file is 100% standalone — it does not touch, reference, or get imported by any
course code in `src/`. Safe to edit freely.

---

## 🎯 The format (read this once, use for every video)

**Recurring host bit:** you, phone propped up, laptop screen visible over your shoulder
or screen-recorded and cut in. Think "senior in the hostel room teaching a junior،" not
"corporate tutorial."

**Every script has 5 beats, timed for 30 seconds:**
| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 🪝 Hook | 0:00–0:03 | Stop the scroll. A question, a bold claim, or a "wait what" moment. |
| ⚡ Setup | 0:03–0:07 | One line: what we're building today. |
| 🛠️ Payoff | 0:07–0:24 | The actual demo/explanation — screen recording is king here. |
| 😄 Punchline | 0:24–0:27 | The fun/relatable twist — this is what makes it a *reel*, not a lecture. |
| 📣 CTA | 0:27–0:30 | "Follow for Part [N]" / "Full course free — link in bio." |

**Recurring catch-phrases** (use 2-3 per video, don't overuse any one):
- *"Guru, ondu card kooda beku illa!"* (Bro, you don't even need one card!)
- *"Kundapura inda Silicon Valley tanaka"* (From Kundapura to Silicon Valley)
- *"Nam ooru style — full free, full solid"* (Our village style — fully free, fully solid)
- *"Idu tutorial alla, idu practical"* (This isn't a tutorial, this is hands-on)
- *"Card illa antha, app illa antha alla!"* (No card doesn't mean no app!)

**Free production kit** (matches the "no card, ever" spirit of the course itself):
phone camera + a free screen recorder (built-in Android/iOS screen record) + **CapCut**
(free) for edits + **VN Editor** (free) as backup. Captions burned in — most people watch
Reels muted.

**Posting cadence:** one every 2 days, same time (e.g. 7 PM IST, after college hours) —
keeps the series feeling like appointment viewing, and gives you time to film Part N+1
using the previous video's comments as inspiration.

## 📋 Quick production checklist (before you film)

- [ ] Screen recorder ready (Android: Settings → built-in; iOS: Control Center)
- [ ] LocalInsta app running on a real device (not emulator — looks more authentic on camera)
- [ ] A few seed posts/photos already in the app so the feed doesn't look empty on camera
- [ ] Phone tripod or propped against something steady for talking-head shots
- [ ] CapCut installed (free) — use the auto-captions feature, then hand-fix the Kannada/English mix
